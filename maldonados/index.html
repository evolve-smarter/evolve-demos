What We Do

Complete Tree Care Services
for Dallas Homeowners

From routine maintenance to emergency removals, our experienced crew handles it all — and hauls away every last branch.

Tree Pruning & Trimming

Keep your trees healthy, shaped, and safe. We remove dead limbs, thin the canopy for air flow, and prune for long-term structure — not just aesthetics.

Tree Removal

Dead, diseased, or dangerously placed trees removed safely and completely. All debris is hauled away — we leave your yard cleaner than we found it.

Stump Grinding

Eliminate unsightly stumps and reclaim your yard. We grind stumps below grade so you can mow, plant, or build right over the spot — no trace left behind.

Emergency Tree Service

Storm damage, fallen limbs, or a tree threatening your home — we respond fast, day or night. Dallas's emergency tree team when you need help right now.

Weight Reduction Over Homes

Heavy canopy over your roof is a liability. We selectively thin and reduce tree weight so overhanging branches no longer put your home at risk during storms.

Fence & Gate Repair

Trees fall on fences. We handle both — tree removal and the fence repair that follows. One crew, one call, no need to coordinate multiple contractors.
